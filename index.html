Getting Started

Adding a Trustline

A trustline takes 30 seconds. The 0.5 XLM reserve is fully refundable when removed.

01

Get a Stellar Wallet

Download Lobstr or Solar, or install Freighter for your browser. Fund it with at least 2 XLM to cover the base reserve.

02

Click + Trustline

Hit the gold + Trustline button on any token card. StellarTerm opens pre-loaded with that exact asset and issuer.

03

Confirm the Operation

Approve the ChangeTrust transaction in your wallet. The 0.5 XLM reserve is locked, not spent — returned when you remove the trustline.

04

Trade on the DEX

Your wallet now holds the slot. Place buy/sell orders on the native Stellar DEX via StellarTerm — no sign-up, no custody.
