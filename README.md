# ⭐ StellarGold

> **Tokenizing Real-World Commodities on the Stellar Network**

StellarGold is a blockchain-focused project built around the **Stellar network**, with a vision of bringing real-world commodities such as **gold, oil, and other valuable assets** into the digital economy through tokenization.

Our goal is to create a simple, transparent, and accessible ecosystem where commodity-focused digital tokens can be issued, transferred, and integrated into modern blockchain applications.

---

## 🌟 Vision

Traditional commodities can be difficult to access, transfer, and integrate into digital financial applications.

**StellarGold** explores how blockchain technology can transform these assets into digital representations that are easier to move, track, and use within a global ecosystem.

> **Real-world value. Digital ownership. Stellar infrastructure.**

---

## 🪙 Token Ecosystem

The StellarGold ecosystem is designed around multiple commodity-focused tokens.

| Asset | Concept |
|---|---|
| 🥇 **Gold Token** | Digital representation of gold-related value |
| 🛢️ **Oil Token** | Digital representation of oil-related value |
| 💎 **Commodity Tokens** | Future tokens connected to additional real-world commodities |

## 🔐 Transparency & Trust

Trust is a core principle of StellarGold.

If a token is marketed as being backed by physical commodities, the project should provide appropriate evidence such as:

- Reserve information
- Independent audits
- Custody details
- Token supply transparency
- Issuance and redemption rules
- Legal and regulatory documentation

StellarGold does **not** make claims about physical backing unless those claims are independently verified and officially documented.
